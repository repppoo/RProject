What is powering this demo?

A multinomial Naive Bayes text classifier trained on 64 hand-written, synthetic English examples. It computes word weights locally; no text is sent to an AI provider. It can miss context, negation, and unfamiliar language, so every output needs human review. “Low signal” does not mean safe.

This is a new portfolio demonstration, separate from my ongoing social-media cyber-threat classification project. The automation really creates local records and exports a CSV; it does not contact a security service or send alerts.
